Refund Procedure

1. Verify the customer order number and purchase date in the billing system.
2. Confirm the item is eligible under the 30-day return policy.
3. Check that the product is unused and in its original packaging.
4. Issue a return authorization number to the customer.
5. Instruct the customer to ship the item to the returns warehouse.
6. Inspect the returned item upon arrival and record the condition.
7. Approve or reject the refund in the billing system within two business days.
8. Refund the original payment method within five to ten business days.
9. Notify the customer by email once the refund has been processed.
10. Record the case outcome in the support ticketing system.

Escalation: contact the finance team if the refund amount exceeds 1,000 USD
or if the original payment method is no longer available.
